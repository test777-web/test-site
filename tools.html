Wheel Offset Calculator

Estimate backspacing from wheel width and offset, or the reverse.

Backspacing = (wheel width / 2) + (offset in mm / 25.4) + 0.5". Standard approximation used by wheel shops, not a substitute for a physical fitment check.

Wheel Fitment: Bolt Pattern Reference

Convert bolt pattern between inches and millimeters, and check common patterns for a few popular platforms.

Reference: Toyota GR86 is 5x100, most modern BMW 3/5-Series are 5x120, Audi A8 is 5x112. Always confirm against your specific model year before buying wheels.

Braking Distance Calculator

Estimate stopping distance including reaction time.

Simplified physics model (v² / 2μg + reaction distance). Real stopping distance depends on tires, brakes, weight, and vehicle condition.
